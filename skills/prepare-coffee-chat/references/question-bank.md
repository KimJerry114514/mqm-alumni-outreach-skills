# 问题库（按对方背景选用，必须结合对方资料改写）

每次只选 5–7 组，Top 4 必问。下列问题是写法示例，不是固定清单。

## 职业路径与转型
- What initially drew you to <field/team>, and what motivated your move to <new role/company>?
- Which parts of your <earlier background> became unexpectedly valuable, and which skills did you deliberately add?
- What was the hardest gap you had to close during that transition?
- Looking back, what helped most in landing your first full-time role: your coursework, internships, research, or personal projects?
- How did you recruit without prior full-time experience? Did recruiters care more about experience or about fit with the role?

## 真实工作内容
- Could you walk me through a typical project, from the original business problem to the final deliverable? Only at the level you're comfortable sharing.
- How much of your day is modeling versus data work, software engineering, testing, or communicating with stakeholders?
- What kinds of business decisions is your team ultimately trying to improve?
- Where does your role sit between <analyst / scientist / engineer>, and where does your responsibility usually end?

## 行业与方法（体现思考深度）
- What makes data science in <industry> different from other industries you've worked in?
- How do you decide whether a problem needs a predictive model versus an experiment or causal approach?
- In a regulated setting like <banking / insurance / healthcare>, how do you trade off accuracy, interpretability, and compliance?
- Which business problems produce measurable value in your setting, and what constraints most change the solution?

## AI 带来的变化
- How is AI changing the work of <analysts / data scientists / engineers> on your team?
- Which parts can realistically be automated, and which still need deterministic logic, validation, or human judgment?
- How do you decide whether a problem needs an LLM or agent-based solution versus traditional ML?
- What separates someone who builds production AI systems from someone who is good at prototyping?

## 招聘信号
- For entry-level candidates, what is usually hardest to find: strong modeling, SQL and experimentation, product intuition, or communication with business stakeholders?
- What should a candidate make sure stands out on the resume, and what separates strong candidates after the resume screen?
- What does the interview process usually look like for this kind of role?
- What makes a portfolio project look credible rather than like a class assignment?
- （对方确有招聘经验时）When you look at a new graduate's portfolio, what makes you think "this person understands how real systems are built"?

## 定位与差距分析（放在后半段）
- Given my background in <…>, what do you see as my strongest transferable advantage and my biggest gap for <role>?
- I'm thinking of positioning myself as "<role> with <capability>". Does that sound coherent, or would you narrow it?
- If you were in my position, what would make the one-sentence story of my background?

## 下一步与人脉（收尾）
- If you were in my position, graduating in <month year> and targeting <role>, what two skills or experiences would you prioritize over the next six months?
- Is there anyone else at <company> or in the alumni network whose perspective you think would be helpful for me?

## 校园与项目类（对方是在校生或刚毕业的学长学姐）
- How did the <TA / club / research> opportunity come about, and what made you a strong candidate?
- Which courses or skills turned out to be most useful in recruiting and interviews?
- Looking back, what is one thing you would do differently if you were starting the program again?
- If you were advising a new student, how would you structure the recruiting timeline from now through graduation?

## If time allows（措辞要软）
- How should I think about <company>'s current sponsorship situation, or who would be the best person to ask?
- Have you seen recent international graduates join your team?
- Are there particular teams or roles at <company> that might fit someone with my background?

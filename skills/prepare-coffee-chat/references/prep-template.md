# 准备文档模板

以下是英文版结构；中文版结构相同，标题可用中文。尖括号内容必须替换为有依据的事实，没有依据就删掉。

---

**Coffee Chat with <Name>**
<Date, time, time zone> · <Zoom / in person at …> · <20–30 min> · <Referred by …, if any>

*Why this person (for me only):* <one or two lines on their unique value>
*Two core questions:* ① <…> ② <…>

**Company & team brief (for me only)**
- <3–5 bullets; mark guesses as "guess — confirm in chat">

**Conversation arc:** <Their Journey → Current Work → … → Who Else to Meet>

**Warm-up**
- <small-talk hook from their profile> — <my related experience, if any>

**Elevator pitch (~40 seconds)**
> Hi <Name>! It's great to meet you, and thanks so much for taking the time. I'm <my name>, currently <program> at <school>, graduating in <month year>. <One-sentence background, chosen for relevance.> I'm now targeting <roles> in <industries>. I was particularly interested in <their specific experience>, so I'd love to learn about <core question 1 in plain words>.

**Q1 · <Heading>** ★ Top 4
<Main question, grounded in their profile>
- Follow-up: <…>
- *Why ask:* <for me only>
- Notes: ______________________

**Q2 · …** (same format; 5–7 groups; mark ★ for the Top 4, "If time allows" for the rest)

*Transition lines between groups:* "That's really interesting. I'm curious how … influences your work at … today."

**If time allows**
- <sponsorship / referral / specific openings, phrased softly>

**Don't ask**
- <question> — <why>

**Closing**
> Thanks so much, <Name>! I really appreciate your perspective, especially <specific takeaway>. I'd love to stay in touch and share some progress with you. Is there anyone else whose perspective you think would be helpful for me?

**After the chat**
- Key insights: ______
- People they suggested: ______
- Follow-ups and dates: ______
- Thank-you note sent within 24 hours: ☐
